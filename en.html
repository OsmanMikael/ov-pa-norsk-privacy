Privacy Policy – Øv på norsk

Version: 1.3 – Current

Updated: 25 November 2026

1. About the app

Øv på norsk is a learning app for adults who want to learn and practise Norwegian at levels A1, A2, B1 and B2.

The app offers reading exercises, listening exercises, practice activities and tests, with explanations in English, Arabic and Ukrainian.

We aim to protect your privacy and minimise the collection of personal data.

3. Using the app without registration

You do not need to create a user account to use Øv på norsk.

The app does not require you to provide:

  • Your name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone number.
  • Date of birth.
  • Address.
  • Payment information.

All learning features are available without registration.

4. Learning progress and local storage

The app stores your learning progress, completed exercises, test results and relevant settings locally on your device.

The app may store information including:

  • Learning progress.
  • Completed exercises.
  • Test results.
  • Language preferences and relevant app settings.
  • The time at which the last full-screen advertisement was displayed.

The time of the last full-screen advertisement is used to limit how frequently such advertisements are displayed.

The app's advertising functionality is configured to display a maximum of one full-screen advertisement every 5 minutes, including after normal app restarts, provided that local storage functions correctly.

This locally stored information is not sent to our exercise database.

Google may additionally store information about privacy choices and consent through its consent solution.

If you delete the app or its stored data, your learning progress, test results and settings may be lost.

Your device's own backups may affect which information is retained or restored.

5. Internet connection and database

Øv på norsk requires an internet connection to function.

The app uses Supabase to retrieve learning exercises and updated educational content.

The primary database is located in Ireland, within the European Economic Area (EEA).

When communicating with the database, technical information may be processed, including:

  • IP address.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Technical request information.
  • Error messages and system logs.

This information may be used to deliver content, maintain security and investigate technical problems.

Read more about Supabase's privacy practices:

https://supabase.com/privacy

6. Advertising

We plan to finance part of the app's operation and further development through advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

The app is configured to request non-personalised advertisements. Limited advertisements may also be used where permitted under applicable privacy legislation and Google's requirements.

The app has two planned advertising formats:

  • Banner advertisements.
  • Full-screen advertisements, which may contain images, text, animations or video.

Banner advertisements may be displayed at the bottom of the following screens:

  • Main screen.
  • Lessons and educational screens.
  • Reading exercises.
  • Listening exercises.
  • Writing exercises.
  • Grammar exercises.
  • Vocabulary exercises.
  • Other regular learning exercises.

We plan to display a maximum of one banner advertisement at a time on the active screen. Advertising content may refresh while the app is being used.

Full-screen advertisements may be displayed when navigating from the main screen to the level menus for:

  • A1.
  • A2.
  • B1.
  • B2.

Full-screen advertisements are configured with a minimum interval of 5 minutes between displays.

Google determines which available advertising content is delivered. A full-screen advertisement may therefore contain video, but not every full-screen advertisement is guaranteed to be a video advertisement.

Users will be able to continue to the learning content after the advertisement has been closed.

Advertisements will be placed separately from learning content, answer options and navigation buttons so that they do not cause accidental clicks.

We do not plan to display advertisements:

  • In test menus.
  • During the actual Norwegian language test.
  • On test results screens.
  • In the Privacy Policy.
  • In consent messages and privacy choices.

Non-personalised advertisements are advertisements that are not selected based on your previous web or app activity.

If you do not consent to processing that requires consent, or if you withdraw your consent, limited advertisements may be used where permitted under applicable privacy legislation and Google's requirements.

Limited advertisements may involve limited processing of technical information, including for advertising delivery, security and prevention of invalid advertising traffic.

Certain technical functions may also involve storing or accessing information on your device. Such functions will only be used where the necessary legal requirements are met.

Limited advertisements are not guaranteed to be available in every situation.

If there is no lawful and technically supported basis for displaying advertisements, the app will not display them.

Google and relevant advertising partners may process technical information in connection with advertising and the consent solution, including:

  • IP address.
  • Approximate geographical location.
  • Device identifiers.
  • Advertising and interaction information.
  • Performance data.
  • Diagnostics and crash logs.
  • Privacy choices and consent information.

This information may be used for purposes including advertising delivery, advertising measurement, security, fraud prevention and technical improvements.

Neither non-personalised nor limited advertising necessarily means that all processing of personal data ceases.

The Android app is configured to remove the Android advertising ID permission. Other technical identifiers may still be processed.

Read more about Google's data processing:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Consent and privacy choices

Øv på norsk has implemented a consent solution based on Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) in the app's source code.

Its purpose is to provide information about advertising-related data processing and allow you to make relevant privacy choices.

The consent solution is undergoing configuration and testing before regular advertising begins.

The consent solution is intended to allow you to:

  • Accept processing that requires consent.
  • Refuse processing that requires consent.
  • Manage available privacy choices.
  • Change or withdraw your consent.

The consent message will be used where required under applicable privacy legislation and Google's requirements, including in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

Although the app is configured to request non-personalised advertisements, consent may still be required for processing personal data or accessing information on your device.

The app checks Google's consent status before sending advertising requests.

If the consent check fails or advertising requests are not permitted, the app will not request new advertisements.

Permission to send advertising requests through Google UMP does not necessarily mean that the user has consented to all forms of advertising-related data processing.

The app will not request or display advertisements that require consent if the necessary consent has not been obtained.

Limited advertisements may only be displayed without such consent where the specific advertising-related processing is permitted under applicable legislation and Google's requirements.

Access to learning exercises, lessons and tests will not depend on your consent to advertising.

Where Google UMP requires privacy choices to be available, the app will provide a dedicated entry point to access those choices.

You will be able to manage available privacy choices and change or withdraw previously given consent through this entry point.

The Privacy Policy will be available both directly within the app and through a publicly accessible website.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

The advertising functionality and consent solution are currently under development and testing. Regular commercial advertising has not been activated.

10. Legal basis for processing

The processing of personal data must have a valid legal basis under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Relevant legal bases include:

  • Processing necessary to provide the service you request, where the applicable conditions are met.
  • Legitimate interests in security, technical operations and handling ordinary support enquiries, following an assessment of the privacy implications.
  • Consent to advertising-related processing and access to information on your device where required.
  • Legal obligations where processing is necessary to comply with legal requirements.

Storing or accessing information on your device must also comply with the applicable rules governing such processing.

The final legal basis for each processing activity will be determined before publication.

11. Data retention

Personal data must not be retained for longer than necessary for the purposes for which it is processed.

  • Learning progress and test results are stored locally on your device.
  • Language preferences and relevant app settings are stored locally.
  • The time of the last full-screen advertisement is stored locally and may be replaced when a new full-screen advertisement is displayed.
  • Supabase's current Free plan has a one-day retention period for API and database logs. This must be confirmed before publication.
  • Ordinary email correspondence is retained for a maximum of 12 months after the matter has been closed, subject to the exceptions described above.
  • Information about privacy choices and consent may be stored through Google UMP.
  • Information processed by Google may have separate retention periods depending on the purpose of processing.

Locally stored information can normally be removed by deleting the app's data or uninstalling the app. Your device's backups may affect this.
