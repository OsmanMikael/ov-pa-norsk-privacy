Privacy Policy – Øv på norsk

Version: 1.4 – Current

Updated: 27 November 2026

1. About the app

Øv på norsk is a learning app for adults who want to learn and practise Norwegian at levels A1, A2, B1 and B2.

The app offers reading exercises, listening exercises, practice activities and tests, with explanations in English, Arabic and Ukrainian.

We aim to protect your privacy and minimise the collection of personal data.

The app is developed for iOS and Android.

3. Using the app without registration

You do not need to create a user account to use Øv på norsk.

The app does not require you to provide:

  • Email address.

Using the app without registration does not necessarily mean that no personal data is processed. Technical information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

4. Learning progress and local storage

The app stores your learning progress, completed exercises, test results and relevant settings locally on your device.

The app may store information including:

  • Learning progress.
  • Completed exercises.
  • Test results.
  • Language preferences and relevant app settings.
  • The time at which the last full-screen advertisement was displayed.

The time of the last full-screen advertisement is used to limit how frequently such advertisements are displayed.

The app's advertising functionality is configured to display a maximum of one full-screen advertisement every 5 minutes, including after normal app restarts, provided that local storage functions correctly.

This locally stored information is not sent to our exercise database.

Google may additionally store information about privacy choices and consent through its consent solution.

On iOS, the operating system also manages the user's tracking preferences through Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT).

If you delete the app or its stored data, your learning progress, test results and settings may be lost.

Your device's own backups may affect which information is retained or restored.

6. Advertising

We plan to finance part of the app's operation and further development through advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

The app is configured to allow Google AdMob to select available advertising types based on relevant privacy choices, consent signals, technical permissions and Google's advertising requirements.

The following advertising types may be available:

PERSONALISED ADVERTISING

Personalised advertisements may be selected based on interests, previous activity and other information used for advertising personalisation.

Such advertisements must only be displayed where the necessary consent, legal requirements and applicable platform permissions are in place.

On iOS, tracking across other companies' apps and websites also requires permission through Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT).

NON-PERSONALISED ADVERTISING

Non-personalised advertisements are not selected based on the user's previous activity to tailor advertisements to the user's interests.

Such advertisements may be based on contextual information, the app's content and approximate geographical location.

Non-personalised advertising may still involve processing personal data and accessing information on your device.

The necessary consent and other legal requirements must therefore be satisfied.

LIMITED ADVERTISING

Where the necessary consent for other advertising-related processing has not been obtained, limited advertisements may be available where the specific processing is permitted under applicable privacy legislation and Google's requirements.

Limited advertising may involve processing technical information for purposes including advertising delivery, security and prevention of invalid advertising traffic.

Certain advertising functions may also involve storing or accessing information on your device.

Such functions must only be used where the necessary legal requirements are met.

Limited advertisements are not guaranteed to be available in every situation.

If there is no lawful and technically supported basis for displaying advertisements, advertisements must not be displayed.

ADVERTISING FORMATS AND PLACEMENT

The app has two planned advertising formats:

  • Banner advertisements.
  • Full-screen advertisements, which may contain images, text, animations or video.

Banner advertisements may be displayed at the bottom of the following screens:

  • Main screen.
  • Lessons and educational screens.
  • Reading exercises.
  • Listening exercises.
  • Writing exercises.
  • Grammar exercises.
  • Vocabulary exercises.
  • Other regular learning exercises.

We plan to display a maximum of one banner advertisement at a time on the active screen.

Advertising content may refresh while the app is being used.

Full-screen advertisements may be displayed when navigating from the main screen to the level menus for:

  • A1.
  • A2.
  • B1.
  • B2.

Full-screen advertisements are configured with a minimum interval of 5 minutes between displays.

Google determines which available advertising content is delivered. A full-screen advertisement may therefore contain video, but not every full-screen advertisement is guaranteed to be a video advertisement.

Users will be able to continue to the learning content after the advertisement has been closed.

Advertisements will be placed separately from learning content, answer options and navigation buttons so that they do not cause accidental clicks.

We do not plan to display advertisements:

  • During the actual Norwegian language test.
  • On test results screens.
  • In the Privacy Policy.

7. Consent and privacy choices

GOOGLE USER MESSAGING PLATFORM (UMP)

Øv på norsk uses a consent solution based on Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Its purpose is to provide information about advertising-related data processing and allow you to make relevant privacy choices.

The consent solution has been implemented and has undergone testing on iOS and Android.

Where available, the consent solution allows you to:

  • Accept processing that requires consent.
  • Refuse processing that requires consent.
  • Manage available privacy choices.
  • View information about relevant advertising partners.
  • Change or withdraw previously given consent.

The consent message is used where required under applicable privacy legislation and Google's requirements, including in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

Both personalised and non-personalised advertising may require consent to process personal data or access information on your device.

The app checks Google's consent status at startup before new advertising requests are permitted.

If the consent check fails or advertising requests are not permitted, the app will not request new advertisements.

Permission to send advertising requests through Google UMP does not necessarily mean that the user has consented to all forms of advertising-related data processing.

Advertisements that require consent must not be displayed if the necessary consent has not been obtained.

Limited advertisements may only be displayed without such consent where the specific advertising-related processing is permitted under applicable legislation and Google's requirements.

APPLE APP TRACKING TRANSPARENCY (ATT)

On iPhone and other supported iOS devices, the app uses Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT).

ATT is Apple's system for giving users control over tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

Where the relevant conditions are met and tracking permission has not previously been determined, the app may display Apple's system dialogue requesting tracking permission.

You may choose to:

  • Allow tracking.
  • Ask the app not to track.

The purpose of any tracking permission is to enable relevant advertising personalisation and measurement of advertising effectiveness where permitted.

If you refuse tracking, the app and its advertising services must respect your choice.

Refusing tracking does not necessarily mean that all advertising or all processing of technical information ceases.

Other forms of advertising may still be available if they do not involve tracking that requires ATT permission and the other applicable legal requirements are met.

You can change tracking permission through your iOS settings:

Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Apple's ATT permission does not replace consent required under data protection legislation.

Consent provided through Google UMP does not replace Apple's ATT permission either.

Both requirements must be respected where they apply to the relevant processing.

Apple ATT is not used on Android.

Read more about Apple's privacy features:

https://www.apple.com/privacy/

CHANGING AND WITHDRAWING CONSENT

Where Google UMP requires privacy choices to be available, the app provides a dedicated entry point to access those choices within the Privacy Policy.

You can manage available advertising choices and change or withdraw previously given consent through this entry point.

The app's advertising functionality is configured to discard previously loaded advertisements when consent status changes and make new advertising requests based on updated privacy choices where such requests are permitted.

On iOS, Apple's tracking permission is managed separately through the device's system settings.

Access to learning exercises, lessons and tests does not depend on your consent to advertising or tracking.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

The Privacy Policy will be available both directly within the app and through a publicly accessible website.

The advertising functionality and consent solutions are currently undergoing testing with test advertisements.

Regular commercial advertising has not been activated.

10. Legal basis for processing

The processing of personal data must have a valid legal basis under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Relevant legal bases include:

  • GDPR Article 6(1)(b): Processing necessary to provide the service you request, where the applicable conditions are met.
  • GDPR Article 6(1)(f): Legitimate interests in security, technical operations and handling ordinary support enquiries, following an assessment of necessity and privacy implications.
  • GDPR Article 6(1)(a): Consent to advertising-related processing, personalisation and other purposes where consent is required.
  • GDPR Article 6(1)(c): Legal obligations where processing is necessary to comply with legal requirements.

Storing or accessing information on your device must also comply with the applicable rules governing such processing, including Section 3-15 of the Norwegian Electronic Communications Act where applicable.

A legal basis under the GDPR does not replace any applicable requirement to obtain consent for storing or accessing information on your device.

Apple's ATT permission is a separate platform permission and does not, on its own, constitute a sufficient legal basis under the GDPR.

The final legal basis for each processing activity, including any advertising-related processing without consent, will be determined before commercial advertising and publication.

Delivery of learning content may fall under Article 6(1)(b). Security, technical operations and user support may fall under Article 6(1)(f), following the necessary balancing assessment. Advertising personalisation requires consent.
